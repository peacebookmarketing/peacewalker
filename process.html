How We Work

Clarity, Precision, and Direct Collaboration

Successful marketing relies on structure and predictability. Over nine years of running book campaigns, I have developed a six-stage methodology that removes guesswork and keeps your publication timeline on schedule.

Because I work independently from the United States, you receive direct, one-on-one communication throughout every stage. We communicate primary details via email, ensuring a clear written record of strategy decisions, asset approvals, and weekly updates.

Whether we are building a brand-new pre-launch sequence or scaling paid ad campaigns for an established backlist series, this disciplined workflow ensures every detail is handled with thorough care.

Review the six core stages below to see exactly what to expect when we partner on your book.

The 6-Stage Framework

Step-by-Step Campaign Roadmap

A systematic approach designed to move your book seamlessly from initial consultation to sustained market presence.

01

Intake & Deep-Dive Market Analysis

Our partnership begins when you reach out via the contact form. I conduct an extensive audit of your manuscript details, target audience profiles, genre landscape, and current retail presence. We establish clear benchmarks, budget parameters, and primary sales goals.

02

Bespoke Strategy & Campaign Architecture

Using data gathered during analysis, I design your customized marketing roadmap. This includes defining your audience targeting parameters, keyword structures, category placements, and multi-channel campaign schedules. I present the complete proposal for your review.

03

Listing & Creative Asset Polish

Before launching paid traffic, we ensure your sales destination is fully optimized. I refine your sales copy blurb, format backend keywords, layout Amazon A+ Content, and prepare high-converting ad creative images and copy variations tailored to your reader demographics.

04

Multi-Channel Campaign Deployment

With assets approved and listing optimized, we initiate campaign deployment. Paid ad campaigns go live across Amazon Ads, Meta, or BookBub based on your selected package. Pre-launch email sequences, promo pricing submissions, and media review requests are executed on schedule.

05

Active Bid Management & Daily Optimization

Marketing requires ongoing calibration. I monitor campaign performance daily, adjusting ad bids, filtering negative search keywords, testing creative variations, and reallocating budget toward the highest-performing target segments to maximize return on ad spend.

06

Performance Review & Backlist Sustenance

At the conclusion of the primary campaign window, you receive a comprehensive performance report detailing ad reach, conversion metrics, and sales growth. We then transition your marketing architecture into an evergreen system to sustain long-term sales momentum.
