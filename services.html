Marketing Capabilities

Bespoke Marketing Solutions for Independent & Published Authors

From high-converting retail listing optimization to precision paid ad campaigns, I deliver strategic, multi-channel solutions that connect your book with dedicated readers.

Strategic Approach

Designed for Long-Term Commercial Success

A successful book marketing strategy is never composed of isolated tactics. Posting on social media without an optimized Amazon store page leads to wasted traffic. Running ads without a clear keyword architecture wastes money. True growth requires an integrated system where every element reinforces the others.

Whether you require a single focused service or an end-to-end multi-phase launch campaign, I bring nine years of practical experience and analytical rigor to every detail. My services are fully customized to align with your genre, publication timeline, and business goals.

I work across fiction and nonfiction categories, serving self-published authors, hybrid publishers, and traditionally published writers who want to take active control of their sales momentum. Every service is delivered directly by me from my base in the United States.

Explore my core capabilities below to see how we can elevate your book's market presence and build a loyal, growing reader base.

Service 01

Pre-Launch Strategy & Audience Positioning

The foundation of a successful book launch is established long before the book is published. I help you define your ideal target audience, analyze competitor benchmarks, and construct a compelling pre-launch runway that generates genuine anticipation.

We optimize your cover presentation, refine your positioning statement, build ARC (Advance Reader Copy) distribution lists, and structure early review sequences so that your book launches with immediate authority.

  • Comprehensive reader persona and demographic profiling
  • Competitive title and genre gap analysis
  • Advance Reader Copy (ARC) team strategy and deployment
  • Pre-order campaign design and email sequence creation

Why Pre-Launch Matters

Retail algorithms favor momentum. Concentrating reader interest during pre-launch ensures strong initial sales velocity, which signals algorithms to begin recommending your book organically.

Service 02

Amazon & Online Retail Listing Optimization

Your book's product page is your most critical sales conversion point. If your book blurb, subtitle, or keywords fail to captivate browsing readers, your marketing budget is wasted.

I perform deep keyword research and category positioning across Amazon, Apple Books, Kobo, and Barnes & Noble. I write compelling, psychology-backed sales copy and structure custom A+ Content that converts casual page visitors into paying readers.

  • In-depth backend keyword research and search term selection
  • Strategic category mapping for maximum bestseller visibility
  • Sales copy rewrite for blurbs, editorial reviews, and bios
  • Amazon A+ Content copy design and layout planning

Maximize Conversion Rates

Even modest improvements to your book description's conversion rate can double the effectiveness of your paid ads without increasing your advertising budget.

Service 03

Paid Advertising Campaigns (Amazon, Meta & BookBub)

Paid advertising is the most controllable, scalable method to drive consistent sales for your book. However, unmanaged ad accounts can quickly drain your resources with minimal return.

I build, launch, and continuously manage high-performing ad campaigns tailored to your genre. By testing target keywords, interest segments, ad copy, and visual creative, I optimize your campaigns for high return on ad spend (ROAS) and sustained sales growth.

  • Amazon Sponsored Products, Brands, and Display ad management
  • Meta (Facebook and Instagram) laser-targeted interest campaigns
  • BookBub Featured Deals and CPM/CPC auction ad campaigns
  • Daily bid management, negative keyword filtering, and budget scaling

Data-Driven Optimization

Ad campaigns are monitored and adjusted continuously based on real sales data, ensuring ad spend is directed toward high-performing reader audiences.

Service 04

Social Media & Author Brand Building

Building a lasting author career requires an authentic brand presence that keeps readers engaged between book releases. I help you construct a streamlined social media and content marketing strategy that feels natural to maintain.

We focus strictly on the platforms where your readers congregate, creating structured content pillars, email newsletter lead magnets, and reader funnel sequences that turn one-time buyers into lifetime fans.

  • Author brand positioning and visual identity guidance
  • Genre-focused social media strategy for BookTok, Instagram, and Goodreads
  • Newsletter lead magnet creation and welcome automation series
  • Content calendar development tailored to your schedule

Owned Audience Control

Social media algorithms change constantly. Building an engaged email list ensures you have a direct, unfiltered line of communication with your readers whenever you launch new work.

Service 05

Book Publicity & Editorial Outreach

Third-party endorsement adds immense credibility to your book. I organize targeted publicity outreach campaigns that connect your title with relevant reviewers, book bloggers, literary podcasts, and media outlets.

By crafting customized pitch letters and identifying tastemakers in your genre, we secure reviews, interviews, and feature mentions that build social proof and expand your organic reach.

  • Tailored press kit and media pitch creation
  • Targeted outreach to genre book bloggers and podcasters
  • Editorial review submission strategy
  • Blog tour coordination and feature placement

Build Lasting Authority

Positive editorial reviews and podcast guest appearances remain discoverable for years, providing evergreen social proof for future readers.

Service 06

Launch Execution & Backlist Sustenance

Launch week demands coordinated multi-channel execution. I orchestrate your entire release schedule, aligning price promotions, ad surges, email broadcasts, and review drops to maximize chart placement.

Following the initial launch window, I transition your campaign into a long-term sustenance strategy designed to maintain ongoing sales velocity and revitalize your existing backlist titles.

  • Full launch week timeline and task orchestration
  • Discount promo site strategy (BookBub, Freebooksy, Bargain Booksy)
  • Backlist cross-promotion and series funnel optimization
  • Post-launch performance reporting and ongoing plan adjustment

Evergreen Growth

A book should continue generating revenue long after its launch week. Sustenance strategies ensure your backlist remains active and profitable over time.
