The Reality

The Gap Between a Finished Manuscript and a Thriving Readership

The publishing landscape has shifted dramatically over the past decade. Traditional gatekeepers no longer hold a monopoly on distribution, and self-publishing platforms have democratised access to global bookstores. This is wonderful news for creative freedom, but it has also created an unprecedented discoverability crisis. With millions of new titles flooding Amazon, Apple Books, Kobo, and Barnes & Noble each year, the average book sells fewer than 250 copies in its entire lifetime.

The problem is rarely the quality of the writing. The problem is visibility. Readers cannot fall in love with a book they never encounter. And in a marketplace saturated with algorithmic noise, paid advertising inflation, and shrinking organic reach on social media, hoping for word-of-mouth alone is no longer a viable strategy. It is a gamble.

This is precisely the challenge I have spent the last nine years solving. My work sits at the intersection of editorial insight, data-driven advertising, brand storytelling, and reader psychology. I study not just how books are sold, but why readers choose one book over another when faced with infinite options. That understanding informs every campaign I build, every ad I optimise, and every launch sequence I design.

Whether you are a debut novelist preparing for your very first release, a seasoned nonfiction author looking to expand into new markets, or a publishing house seeking an external strategist to amplify a seasonal catalogue, I bring the same level of rigour, creativity, and personal attention to every project I take on.

What I Do

Comprehensive Marketing Services Tailored to Your Book

I offer a full spectrum of book marketing services designed to meet you exactly where you are in your publishing journey. Each service can be engaged independently or woven together into a cohesive, multi-phase campaign that carries your book from pre-launch anticipation through long-term reader acquisition.

01

Pre-Launch Strategy & Audience Building

The most successful book launches begin months before publication day. I work with you to identify your core reader demographics, develop a compelling author brand narrative, build anticipation through strategic content sequencing, and establish the foundational marketing assets including your book description, keyword architecture, and category positioning that will determine your discoverability for years to come.

02

Amazon & Retail Optimisation

Your book's product page is your most important sales asset, and most authors leave enormous revenue on the table by neglecting its optimisation. I conduct deep-dive audits of your Amazon listing including title metadata, subtitle keywords, category selection, A+ Content design, backend search terms, and pricing strategy to ensure your book surfaces when readers are actively searching for books like yours.

03

Paid Advertising Campaigns

I design, launch, and continuously optimise paid advertising campaigns across Amazon Ads, Meta (Facebook and Instagram), BookBub, and Google to drive targeted traffic directly to your book's purchase page. Every campaign is built on granular audience segmentation, rigorous A/B testing, and real-time bid management to maximise your return on ad spend. The focus is always on actual sales and measurable reader growth, not just impressions.

04

Social Media & Content Marketing

Building a genuine and engaged readership requires far more than sporadic posts. I develop comprehensive social media strategies that position you as a compelling voice in your genre, create shareable content calendars aligned with your launch timeline, and cultivate authentic community engagement across the platforms where your ideal readers already spend their time, whether that is BookTok, Bookstagram, Goodreads, or beyond.

05

Publicity, Reviews & Media Outreach

Credibility accelerates sales. I craft targeted media pitches, coordinate review campaigns with influential book bloggers and editorial reviewers, and pursue strategic placements in podcasts, literary magazines, and genre-specific publications that put your book in front of the readers and tastemakers most likely to champion it and spread the word organically.

06

Launch Execution & Post-Launch Sustenance

Launch week is a critical window, but the real work begins after the initial spike. I orchestrate multi-channel launch sequences designed to concentrate sales velocity during the algorithmically crucial first 30 days, then transition seamlessly into evergreen marketing systems that sustain visibility, drive consistent daily sales, and build your backlist performance over the long term.

My Philosophy

Marketing Is an Extension of Storytelling

Too many book marketers approach their work as a purely technical exercise, a matter of keywords, bid strategies, and algorithmic manipulation. While those elements are undeniably important, I believe they are secondary to a more fundamental truth: the best book marketing is itself a form of storytelling. It is about crafting a narrative around your book that resonates so deeply with your ideal reader that purchasing feels less like a transaction and more like a discovery.

When I develop a campaign for your book, I begin by immersing myself in your manuscript, your author story, and the emotional core of what you have written. I identify the themes, tensions, and promises that will most powerfully attract your target audience, and I build every piece of marketing collateral from your Amazon blurb to your ad creatives to your social media voice around that authentic narrative foundation.

This philosophy is what separates a forgettable marketing campaign from one that transforms an unknown author into a recognised name within their genre. Readers today are sophisticated. They can sense when a book is being aggressively sold to them, and they instinctively resist it. But when they encounter marketing that speaks to their desires, their frustrations, and their aspirations with genuine understanding, they lean in. They click. They read the sample. They buy. And most importantly, they tell their friends.

That is the kind of marketing I create. Not noise, but signal. Not interruption, but invitation. If that resonates with you, I would love to hear about your book and explore how we can bring it to the readers who are already searching for it.
